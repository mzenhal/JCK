# JCK Builder's & Interior LLP – Premium Website Demo Specification

## Project Goal

Design and develop a premium, modern, professional website for **JCK Builder's & Interior LLP**, a company specializing in:

* Building Construction
* Architectural Planning
* Interior Design
* Renovation & Remodeling
* Commercial & Residential Projects

The website should create a strong first impression, build trust, showcase expertise, and generate leads.

---

# Design Direction

## Visual Style

* Premium and elegant appearance
* Modern architecture-inspired design
* Clean layouts with plenty of white space
* Avoid cluttered sections
* Focus on large visuals and storytelling

## Theme

### Primary Colors

* Deep Navy Blue (#0F172A)
* Gold Accent (#D4AF37)
* White (#FFFFFF)

### Secondary Colors

* Light Gray
* Charcoal Black

### Typography

* Modern professional fonts
* Bold headings
* Clean readable body text

---

# Visual Experience

## Hero Section

Full-screen landing section featuring:

* High-quality cinematic construction video background
* Slow camera movement effect
* Premium overlay
* Animated headline

Example:

"Building Excellence. Designing Dreams."

Buttons:

* Get Free Consultation
* View Projects

---

## 3D Visual Elements

Use subtle 3D assets throughout the website:

* Modern building renders
* Interior design mockups
* Architectural models
* Floating geometric design elements

Animations should be smooth and professional.

Avoid flashy effects.
